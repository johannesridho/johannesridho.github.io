Ceki is an application that simplify payment. The idea is to use QR codes for any products and then the user can just scan it to pay. This application was selected as the 2nd place winner in BNI Hackfest Malang, top 10 in [Bank Rakyat Indonesia Hackathon 2016](https://id.techinasia.com/ide-startup-terbaik-di-hackathon-bri-digital-challenge), and top 10 at Jenius BTPN Hackathon 2017.
